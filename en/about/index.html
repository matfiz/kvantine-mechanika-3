About the course

Quantum Mechanics III: Foundations

Fourteen lectures on the foundations of quantum theory: states, symmetry, composite and open systems, observation and the measurement problem.

Prerequisites

  • Linear Algebra for Physics
  • Quantum Mechanics I: Finite Systems
  • Quantum Mechanics II: Continuous Systems

How to study

Lectures divide the material by topic; a lecture need not be completed in one sitting. The sequence moves from states to symmetry and evolution, parts and channels, apparatus models and the measurement problem. The final lectures compare proposals that revise different premises of that problem.

Read the lectures in order. Attempt the self-checks before revealing the answers. Proofs state their hypotheses; sketches and cited results are marked. Exercise sheets focus on computations and applications.

Checked learning outcomes are your own assessment. They are saved in this browser profile and shared across the two course languages. My progress lets you view and transfer those records.

Editions

Lectures and exercise sheets are available in Lithuanian and English. Use the complete notes for a first read. The core edition is for reference afterwards; it omits the physics boxes and self-check material.
